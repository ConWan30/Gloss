Marks

Each reading carries one mark. Shape and color both say it, so a mark reads in a glance and in grayscale.

open

Alleged by the room. Nothing in the window binds it yet.

bound

Window facts support it. No facts, never Bound. A citation is not Bound.

thin

A claim with little in the window to carry it.

split

More than one live reading on the same window.

clash

Support and contradiction both high. Two pressures. Neither is crowned.

echo

The same reading in other words. Identity, not proof.

closed

Out of the live three. Kept in the folio.

hold

Held by the operator for tape. The caption shows only this.

How the margin behaves

Rules from the constitution. The surfaces follow them; the design only makes them visible.

Three live readings

Opening a fourth is forbidden. Merge or close first. Empty places show as vacant slots.

Bound needs the window

Bound requires window facts. Score, clock, down, and lead are claims of type state.

□An honest empty frame

The caption is the held live reading. No Hold, it shows □. Nothing is filled in.

Glass is off by default

On, a reading may show clock_ns and frame_seq from a tapped window. Missing, malformed, or same_seq false renders □. A citation is not evidence.

Second is a bind

A viewer binds to a live reading. Not a vote. Not a new slot.
